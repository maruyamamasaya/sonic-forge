# Natural wood UI materials

Walnut, white oak and cherry PNG textures were generated with the built-in image generation tool and approved during the 2026-10-17 SonicForge UI design discussion. They are synthetic material images, not photographs of a specific product.

Shared prompt: front-facing orthographic continuous wood surface, vertical natural grain, even diffuse illumination, realistic fibers and pores, matte clear oil finish, no text, logos, seams, objects, prominent knots, varnish glare or painted grain. Species directions: medium warm gray-brown American black walnut; pale honey-beige European white oak with open pores; warm reddish golden-brown American cherry with fine closed pores.

The app bundles these files locally through scripts/build.sh. WoodSurface crops each image to its panel without relying on seamless tiling. Original generated PNGs are retained at full resolution. Theme choice is an app preference, independent of instrument and ensemble documents.
